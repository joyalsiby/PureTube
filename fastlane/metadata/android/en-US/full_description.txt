PureTube is a 100% ad-free, distraction-free Material 3 YouTube video player built for parents, kids, and mindful focus.

Keep the official YouTube app locked on your phone and share or paste only the exact video link you want your child (or yourself) to watch:
• Zero Ads & Zero Tracking: Powered locally by NewPipeExtractor and AndroidX Media3 (ExoPlayer).
• No Search Bar, No Recommendations, No Shorts Feed: Prevents kids and students from falling into algorithmic rabbit holes.
• Kids & Focus Parental Controls: Start videos automatically with Screen Lock enabled so toddlers cannot accidentally tap out, auto-enter Fullscreen, or hide descriptions.
• Full YouTube Player Features: Up to 4K HDR/60fps quality, Multi-Audio Track switcher (defaults to Original audio), Closed Captions (CC), Video Chapters, Clickable Timestamps, Pinch-to-Zoom, Fullscreen Brightness/Volume swipe gestures, Background Audio Play, and Picture-in-Picture (PiP).
• Built-In App Updater: Automatically checks GitHub Releases and updates in-app with one tap.
